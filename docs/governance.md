# Governance and delegation

## Structure

```text
Chairman
└── Board / Foundation governance (trustees or society members, per the verified legal form)
    └── President
        └── Director General
            └── Group Director
                ├── Institution heads (principals/directors)
                └── Functional heads (finance, HR, admissions, T&P, IT, compliance, marketing, facilities)
```

## Reserved matters (chairman approval)

- Capex or commitments above a threshold set by the board (proposal: ₹25 lakh)
- New institution, programme, campus or industry
- Borrowing above ₹50 lakh
- Senior appointments: institution head and above
- Major partnerships and MoUs with financial terms
- Material litigation
- Regulator show-cause notices or adverse inspections
- Brand or reputation crises

## RACI for the admissions loop

| Activity | Responsible | Accountable | Consulted | Informed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Weekly funnel data | Admissions office | Head of Admissions | Institution heads | APEX lead |
| AMBER response | Head of Admissions | Director General | Marketing | Chairman (weekly review) |
| RED decision | Director General | Chairman | Finance, institution head | Board pack |
| Overrides of forecasts | Proposer | Director General | APEX lead | Logged |

## Decision cadence

| Frequency | Output |
|---|---|
| Daily | Chairman brief |
| Weekly | Operating review |
| Monthly | Board pack and risk register |
| Quarterly | Capital allocation and competitor benchmark |
| Annually | Strategy, budget and portfolio review |

Decision turnaround time is tracked from the decision register (proposed → decided).
