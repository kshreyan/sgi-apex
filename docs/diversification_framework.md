# Diversification framework (Phases 8–10, gated)

## Core Health Gate

Before any aggressive diversification, compute a core health score from:
- liquidity and debt
- seat fill
- student outcomes and placements
- faculty stability
- compliance and accreditation
- management depth
- hospital quality
- cash generation

**If any threshold fails, the answer is "fix the core first".**

## Legal separation

A charitable or educational foundation may be restricted from running unrelated commercial businesses. Before any venture, get professional legal and tax review of:
- the charitable objects
- Sections 11–13 of the Income-tax Act (charitable exemption) and related-party rules
- the right vehicle (separate company or SPV)

Funds are never commingled without verified legal authority.

## Venture stage-gates

| Gate | Stage |
|---|---|
| 0 | Idea |
| 1 | Market evidence |
| 2 | Business model |
| 3 | Pilot |
| 4 | Unit economics proven |
| 5 | Regional scale |
| 6 | National scale |

Kill criteria are written down **before** investment.

## Opportunity ranking

Industries are ranked on:
- market size, growth and profit pool
- capital intensity, regulation, competition and entry barriers
- talent needs and technology disruption
- SGI capability fit, brand fit, distribution, geography and synergy
- cash generation and downside

Candidates come from a market search, not from assumption.

## International

Start low-capital: academic, industry and research partnerships, plus exchanges. Commit large capital only after domestic repeatability is proven.
