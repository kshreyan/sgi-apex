# F. Strategic gap analysis: what stands between SGI and national quality

Based on public evidence only. Each item is a hypothesis to test with internal data.

| Gap | Evidence | Why it matters | First move |
|---|---|---|---|
| **No external quality signal** | No NAAC, NBA or NIRF participation found in public sources | National credibility is benchmarked on these. Without them SGI is invisible to national comparisons | AISHE completeness → NAAC readiness self-study for the oldest eligible institution |
| **Evidence hygiene** | Accreditation page lists 5 regulators with no certificate; one link points to the wrong PDF | Regulators, parents and employers read this page. It is also the seed of the compliance graph | Document-evidence audit, then the compliance register (M2–M3) |
| **Identity sprawl** | Two Instagram handles, two YouTube handles, misspelt microsite footer, historical names unresolved, hospital unattributed | Brand trust and search consolidation | One brand architecture: SGI master brand plus school sub-brands |
| **Outcome transparency** | Placements shown as individual testimonials, not cohort rates | National peers publish placement rate, median salary and higher-study share | Placement register with cohort denominators (M3) |
| **Research base** | No visible publications or funded projects | NIRF/NAAC weight research and faculty quality | Faculty profile and publication register. Pick 1–2 clusters (pharmacy and nursing are natural) |
| **Data infrastructure** | Static website forms; no visible ERP | None of the forecasting in this spec works without trusted operational data | Phase 1 single source of truth: admissions and fees first |
| **Healthcare–education linkage** | Nursing and pharmacy schools exist; hospital link unverified | A verified teaching-hospital ecosystem is a real moat for health programmes | Verify entity, then clinical-placement capacity model |

## Candidate competitive moats (to validate)

1. **Health-education ecosystem.** Nursing, pharmacy and a hospital, if verified.
2. **Affordable quality in Siwan / West Champaran.** Low-competition districts combined with BSCC financing.
3. **Bihar recruitment network.** Feeder schools across north Bihar.

**Do not expand yet.** Expansion screening (new campus, new industry) stays switched off until the Core Health Gate is computed on real data.
