# Chairman operating model

APEX exists so that the chairman is hard to surprise and spends time on strategy, capital, leadership, governance, quality, reputation and partnerships, not on chasing reports.

## The five questions every screen answers

1. What changed?
2. What will happen?
3. What needs attention?
4. What decision is required?
5. Who owns it?

## Compression rule

The default view shows **5–15 items**. An item appears only when it is both **likely** (probability threshold) and **material** (≥3 seats or ≥5% of intake, for admissions). Everything else is counted under "no action required".

## Every item carries

Issue, impact, probability, trend, why, options, recommendation, confidence (ROBUST / MODERATE / FRAGILE), owner and deadline. Each statement is tagged **FACT**, **FORECAST**, **ASSUMPTION** or **RECOMMENDATION**.

## Exception levels

| Level | Meaning | Who sees it |
|---|---|---|
| GREEN | Normal | Counted only |
| AMBER | Executive attention | Head of function |
| RED | Chairman awareness or action | Daily brief |
| BLACK | Critical legal, safety or liquidity event | Immediate escalation. Raised only by compliance, finance or safety engines |

## Learning

Decisions are judged by **quality at the time**, not only by outcome. A 90%-probability call that fails is logged as a good decision with a bad outcome. Overrides are scored against models.
